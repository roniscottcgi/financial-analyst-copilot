# Example Business Questions by Use Case

## Financial Analyst Copilot
- What was recognized revenue and gross margin by product family for fiscal month 4?
- Which customers had the largest margin decline period over period?
- What KPI definition should be used for Gross Margin and who owns it?
- Which periods are still open and may affect reported totals?
- Show revenue trend for Enterprise segment customers across the last 3 periods.

## Customer Support Automation
- How many open P1 and P2 tickets exist by issue category?
- Which customers have the most VPN Access tickets this month?
- What is the event history for ticket 1001?
- Which products generate the highest refund-related ticket volume?
- What policy article summaries are relevant to a billing question workflow?

## Internal Knowledge Assistant
- List all Internal Knowledge and Policy domain articles effective after 2025-06-01.
- Which use cases have the highest confidence scores in workflow_feedback?
- What feedback themes suggest retrieval or grounding quality issues?
- Which articles should be surfaced first for access request workflows?
- How does adoption differ by actor_role across use cases?

## IT Service Desk Agentic Assist
- What is the current status, priority, and event history for a password reset ticket?
- Which agent team handles the highest volume of Device Compliance issues?
- What support policies are most relevant to VPN or password reset workflows?
- How many tickets ended with Escalated or Manual Review outcomes?
- Which workflow feedback entries indicate low confidence and need fallback improvements?
